How it works

No application. No tier requirement.

Connect a wallet to claim your code. No node purchase required. Share it, and earn on every sale it brings in.

01

Sign in, claim your code

A unique referral code is issued the moment you sign in with a wallet — no purchase, no registration form, no tier gate. All five cascade levels are open to anyone with a code. Self-referral is blocked.

02

Your buyer gets 10% off

Anyone who checks out using your code receives an instant 10% discount, enforced by smart contract. Independent of — and never deducted from — your commission.

03

Paid biweekly in USDC

All five levels settle directly to your wallet on a fortnightly cadence. Post-TGE, the cascade transitions to ongoing $OPRN emissions from a dedicated pool.

Common questions

Quick answers.

Everything else lives in the full FAQ.

When are commissions paid?→

Pre-TGE: USDC, biweekly, directly to your wallet. Post-TGE: ongoing $OPRN bonus emissions from the Referral & Distribution Pool.

Where does the funding come from?→

A dedicated 10% of total $OPRN supply (4.2B tokens) is reserved for the Referral & Distribution Pool. Commissions are never deducted from buyer purchases or node earnings.

Do I need to hold a node to earn?→

No. Sign in with a wallet, get a referral code, share it. All five cascade levels (L1–L5) are open to anyone with a code — no node purchase required.

How do I apply for Elite Partner?→

Email contact@operon.network with a brief introduction, your audience or capability, and any prior distribution track record. The programme is invitation-only — eligible applicants are reviewed within 5 business days.
